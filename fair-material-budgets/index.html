How it was calculated

1 space = full space − used space
2 Spain = space × Spain's people ÷ world's people
2b ability to pay = Spain × world GDP per head ÷ Spain GDP per head
3 buildings = Spain × buildings' share of that pressure
3b human needs swaps in the needs share
4 material = buildings × group share × share in group
5 tonnes = material ÷ impact per tonne
5b binding budget = lowest tonnes across all limits
6 overshoot = demand ÷ binding budget
